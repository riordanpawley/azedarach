# dec-22: Resolve editable-field conflicts through ordered patches and recoverable supersession

- Created: 2026-07-14
- Updated: 2026-07-14

## Rationale

Let disjoint field patches coexist, order accepted same-field patches through the project authority, retain superseded values, notify affected editors, and provide restoration. Reject stale structured edits that cannot be safely expressed as independent patches.

## Context

A small team benefits from low-friction editing, but silent loss of meaningful text or policy changes would undermine trust.

## Consequences

Ordinary same-field overwrites do not require a modal merge dialog. Structured conflicts return typed actionable outcomes with edit-and-retry, discard, or restore-as-draft paths.
