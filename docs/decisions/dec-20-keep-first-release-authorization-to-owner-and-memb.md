# dec-20: Keep first-release authorization to owner and member

- Created: 2026-07-14
- Updated: 2026-07-14

## Rationale

Give members ordinary access to shared semantic records and participation in review and GitHub-backed integration. Reserve membership administration, project policy, recovery, retirement, and exceptional override for owners.

## Context

The initial audience is one small trusted developer team, not a multi-team enterprise organization.

## Consequences

The project must always retain at least one owner. Ownership transfer is explicit. Removing a member revokes their devices, invitations, provider credentials, and active authorization while preserving historical attribution.
